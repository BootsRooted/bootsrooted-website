Our Mission

Building Farms.
Building Farmers.

Boots Rooted trains and empowers individuals to design, build, and operate sustainable farms while simultaneously creating functioning farms that serve communities. We believe in hands-on learning, real-world impact, and a future where more people have access to land, food, and the skills to be self-sufficient.

Learn More About Our Mission →
Our Story

From Service to Sustainability

Boots Rooted began with a commitment to help veterans find purpose through hands-on agriculture. That vision has grown into a program open to everyone. We bring people together to learn practical skills while building farms that can serve their communities for years to come.
