Frequently Asked Questions

Good Questions.
Real Answers.

Is the program free for veterans?

Yes. Boots Rooted plans to offer tuition-free training for veterans.

Do I need farming experience?

No prior farming experience is required to express interest. The program emphasizes learning by doing.

Where is training held?

Training takes place at participating host farm sites. Specific locations will be shared for each cohort.

How long is the program?

The planned core program runs 12 weeks, Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 3 PM. Dates are announced separately.

How do I apply?

Use the Apply / Express Interest link on this page to complete the interest form.
